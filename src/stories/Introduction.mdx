# Agreed design system

Agreed turns a recorded two-person discussion into its final commitments, each backed by a quote you can play.
This Storybook holds every piece of its interface: tokens, icons, components and full pages. It renders them with real results from the test recordings, so what you see here is what the product shows.

## Style

Editorial and blunt: forest green, cream and one acid-lime accent; square corners, 2 px lines, no shadows;
heavy uppercase headlines in Onest, Geist for text, Geist Mono for labels.

## Principles

1. **Evidence first.** Every claim can be checked: a word-for-word quote, its time in the recording and a ▶ button.
2. **Final state, visible history.** The card shows how things ended. How they got there is one click away.
3. **Unknown is a state.** “No owner named” and “date context missing” have their own look. They are answers, not errors.
4. **One accent, and colour never works alone.** Lime marks the main action and agreed items, violet open questions, orange what needs a person. There is always an icon or a word next to the colour.
5. **One main action per screen.**
6. **Nothing sticky over content.** Panels scroll on their own; nothing floats over the text.

## Rules for building screens

- Screens are composed only from these components. A new element appears here first, then on a screen.
- Colours, type sizes and spacing come only from tokens (`src/styles/tokens.css`, `src/styles/app.css`). No raw hex values or arbitrary Tailwind values; layouts use named grids.
- Icons only through `<Icon name="…">` and its map. **No emoji in the interface.**
- A component is done when it has a story for every state, passes the accessibility check, works at 390 px and in the dark theme.

Use the toolbar to switch the theme and the viewport (390, 768, 1024, 1440).
