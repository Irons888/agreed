# Spacing, lines, surfaces

A 4 px step.

<SpacingScale />

## Lines and surfaces

No rounded corners and no shadows. Edges are square, separation is a 2 px line, and depth comes from switching surfaces:
cream page, paper cards, forest bands (`surface-dark`), a lime accent. Dashed lines mean “not counted”; a 4 px lime ring marks the item you are pointing at in the transcript.

<LinesAndSurfaces />

## Layout

- Breakpoints: 390 (phone), 768, 1024, 1440. Content is at most 1152 px wide.
- Side padding: 16 px on phones, 24 on tablets, 32 on desktops.
- Results: two columns from 1024 px (items 7 : transcript 5, `grid-split`), one column below.
- Named grids instead of arbitrary values: `grid-split`, `grid-hero`, `grid-facts`, `grid-turn`.
- Controls are 36, 44 or 56 px high. On phones anything tappable is at least 44 px.

## Motion

120 ms for hover, 200 ms for expanding, 320 ms for screen changes, all on `cubic-bezier(.2, 0, 0, 1)`.
Two loops: the marquee on the home page and the bars on a playing timestamp. With `prefers-reduced-motion` both stop.
