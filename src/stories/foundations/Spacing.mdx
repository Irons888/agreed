## Layout

- Breakpoints: 390 (phone), 768, 1024, 1440. Content is at most 1152 px wide.
- Side padding: 16 px on phones, 24 on tablets, 32 on desktops.
- Results: two columns from 1024 px (items 7 : transcript 5), one column below.
- Controls are 32, 36 or 44 px high. On phones anything tappable is at least 44 px.

## Motion

120 ms for hover, 200 ms for expanding, 320 ms for screen changes, all on `cubic-bezier(.2, 0, 0, 1)`.
With `prefers-reduced-motion` animations are switched off.
