# Content

Plain English, short sentences, sentence case. The interface states facts and never guesses.

## Terms

| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| Agreed task | Someone committed to it, or accepted a request |
| Open question | Asked and still unanswered at the end |
| Needs review | Hedged or unclear; not counted until confirmed |
| Not accepted | Proposed, nobody accepted |
| Cancelled | Agreed, then called off |
| Owner | The person who committed or accepted |
| Evidence | The quotes an item is based on |

## Formats

| What | Format | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Position in a recording | m:ss | 0:41 |
| Processing time | seconds, one decimal | 16.3 s |
| Money | four decimals under a cent | $0.0084 · $0.0071/min |
| Calendar date | weekday, month, day | Fri, Oct 9 |
| Spoken wording | curly quotes, exactly as said | “by Wednesday” |

## Unknowns

- **No owner named** — nobody took the task.
- **No deadline agreed** — no date or time was agreed.
- **Date context missing** — a weekday or “tomorrow” was said, but the recording doesn't say what day it was.
- **Speaker 1, Speaker 2** — the voices never said their names.

## Refusals and errors

Say what was detected, then what to do: “Detected 3 voices. This prototype supports conversations between two people.”
