# Colors

Forest green and cream carry the page; a softened lime is the one accent. Values are read live from `tokens.css`.
Dark bands (`surface-dark`) and the dark theme in the toolbar redefine the same tokens, so components never check which surface they are on.

## Statuses

Each status is a solid tag: text, fill and line, always with an icon and a word. Lime for agreed, violet for open questions, orange for what needs a person, grey for what is not counted.

## Speakers

Lime and forest on light surfaces, lime and cream on dark ones: they differ in lightness, not only in hue, so they stay apart for colour-blind readers.
Speaker colours appear only on avatars, the left rule of quotes and the waveform, and always next to a name or initial.
